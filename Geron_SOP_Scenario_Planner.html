🔗 Combined Pool — LP + DP Scenario Planning
Treats 188mg LP + 200mg DP (or 47mg + 50mg) as a single pooled inventory of finished drug material. LP and DP are the same drug at different packaging stages, so both contribute to the pool. Only LP patient sales deplete the pool. MRP schedules additional DP batches when the pool projects below the target MOH.
